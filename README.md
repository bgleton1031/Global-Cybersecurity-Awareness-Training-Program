# Global-Cybersecurity-Awareness-Training-Program
Scalable cybersecurity awareness program designed to reduce human risk and strengthen security culture. Features role-based modules, phishing simulation concepts, microlearning strategy, and delivery models adaptable for SMB, mid-market, and enterprise environments aligned to governance and compliance.


A structured security awareness initiative designed to reduce human risk and align workforce behavior with organizational security policies and compliance expectations.

This program is built to:

* Increase employee awareness of evolving cyber threats

* Reduce phishing susceptibility and risky behaviors

* Reinforce secure habits across distributed teams

* Support compliance, governance, and risk management objectives 

Key Design Components:

* Role-based training modules tailored to job functions

* Real-world phishing simulations and threat scenarios

* Monthly microlearning to reinforce secure behaviors

* Executive-level briefings to align leadership with risk posture 

Delivery Model:

* Instructor-led live sessions with real-time Q&A

* Recorded on-demand modules for global scalability

* Scenario-based demonstrations of real attack methods 

💡 Why this matters:
Security awareness is treated as an operational control, not just a compliance checkbox—turning employees into an active layer of defense (“human firewall”).
